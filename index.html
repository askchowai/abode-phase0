What makes an address page different

✓ Verified owners

Match to the county deed, confirm by mailed code or title document, then post as the owner of record. The badge is worth something because it is hard to get.

A permanent record

Renovations, roof replacements, storm claims, price cuts, who built it and when. The listing expires. The history does not.

Neighbors who were there

The tenant who knows the furnace. The kid who grew up there and knows where the creek goes. Upvoted to the top, downvoted out of the way.

Offers in the open

Put a number and your terms in front of the owner directly. Non-binding until a licensed broker writes it up — but no more guessing what the house is really worth.
