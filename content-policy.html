Content policy

Every address on Abode has a permanent public page. That is the product, and it is also the risk: a page about a house is a page about the people in it unless we draw a hard line. Here is the line.

What belongs on an address page

  • The building: what was built, replaced, repaired, flooded, permitted or neglected.
  • The transaction: listings, price changes, offers, sales, who represented whom.
  • The block: traffic, drainage, noise, utilities, what the street does in a storm.
  • First-hand experience of living there, from anyone who did.

What does not

  • Occupants. No names, routines, schedules, working hours, holidays, or whether a house is empty. An address page must never be a burglary index. This is blocked at the point of writing.
  • Protected-class preferences. See fair housing.
  • Claims about people rather than property. "The foundation was repaired badly" is a claim about a house and is allowed. "The owner is a crook" is a claim about a person and is not.
  • Contact details in public. Use a direct message.
  • Listing spam. Agents post under their own name with their brokerage, or not at all.

Reports and appeals

Moderators work from a queue. Anyone can report a post. A moderator reads every report — the author is told a report exists and what rule it cites, never who filed it. Decisions are: leave it up, remove it, or remove it and act on the account. The author can appeal once, and the appeal goes to a different moderator.

Owners and the record

A verified owner can pin one post, answer anything on their wall, and ask for review of a post they believe is defamatory. An owner cannot delete criticism of their house. When the deed changes hands, the badge follows it; the previous owner keeps a past-owner badge, and their posts stay up.

Removal

Removed content is retained in a moderation log for one year for appeals and legal process, then deleted. A takedown by court order is honoured, and noted on the page as a removal rather than silently disappearing.

This is a demonstration build. Moderation described here is modelled in the interface; no human moderators are on duty.
